Preparing American rowers for success — today and tomorrow

TRC - High Performance

Our mission is to support and expand access to rowing at Texas Rowing Center in Austin, Texas, by providing financial assistance and resources to young athletes pursuing excellence in the sport. We seek to ensure that financial circumstances do not prevent young rowers from participating fully in training, competitions, and regattas at the local, state, and national levels.

By providing meals, scholarships, equipment support, and assistance with competition-related expenses—including regatta fees, travel, lodging, and meals—we help support opportunities for young athletes to develop their skills, character, discipline, teamwork, and confidence through the sport of rowing.

Stop 1

Origins

Texas Rowing Center, located in Austin, Texas, started its after-school juniors rowing program in 2003 to introduce the sport of rowing to young Texans.

Our goal is to develop team boats capable of competing in regattas and winning state and national championships. Our program has also placed athletes on the U.S. Junior National Team and the 2024 US Olympic Team. Many of our athletes continue to row in college.

Stop 2

Mission

TRC High Performance is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation established in 2021.

Today, TRC High Performance’s mission is to support junior athletes at TRC by providing financial assistance and resources to ensure that financial circumstances do not prevent young rowers from participating fully in training, competitions, and regattas at the local, state, and national levels.

By providing meals, scholarships, equipment support, and assistance with competition-related expenses—including regatta fees, travel, lodging, and meals—we help support opportunities for young athletes to develop their skills, character, discipline, teamwork, and confidence through the sport of rowing.

Stop 3

Values

Dedication.Humility.Respect.

TRC HP expects dedication, humility, and respect from all athletes, coaches, and staff. We believe that an environment that is supportive of every party will produce the best people and the best results.

TRC High Performance is committed to providing each athlete with everything they need to succeed at the highest level.

Some of our athletes may receive limited funding directly from USRowing, but it is not nearly enough to cover everything that training like a professional athlete entails. And USRowing does not currently support TRC HP financially in any way.

TRC HP pays for all of the athletes’ costs incurred during racing throughout the year, the world-class equipment they use throughout the year, the best coaching in the United States, and physical and mental support services such as physical therapy and mental performance coaching.

All together, this support allows our athletes to have less stress about money AND reduce their hours at work, so they spend more time and energy on the one thing that earns medals: training.
